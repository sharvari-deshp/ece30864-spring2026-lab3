Some positives of Agile developement would be its huge focus on customer value, because Agile attends to their preferences a lot. Also, the fact that there can be a lot of stakeholder collaboration with this software. Some cons include that it is good for medium-sized teams, but more difficult for bigger teams to implement and use. 
The scrum master guides team members on working on the project, and is more on the management side. Their responsibilities include providing goals/ideas, and monitoring the scrum team. The product owner has responsibilities regarding the actual final product, and making sure that it is up to date in terms of priorities. Responsibilites include having a role in marketing, and maximizing the value of the product.
