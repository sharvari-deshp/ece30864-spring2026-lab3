Some positives of Agile developement would be its huge focus on customer value, because Agile attends to their preferences a lot. Also, the fact that there can be a lot of stakeholder collaboration with this software. Some cons include that it is good for medium-sized teams, but more difficult for bigger teams to implement and use. 
The scrum master guides team members on working on the project, and is more on the management side. Their responsibilities include providing goals/ideas, and monitoring the scrum team. The product owner has responsibilities regarding the actual final product, and making sure that it is up to date in terms of priorities. Responsibilites include having a role in marketing, and maximizing the value of the product. 
Three types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle would be sprint planning, daily scrum, and sprint review. Within sprint planning, two points that need to be discussed would be running through the prioritized backlog, and the developers, testers, and designers breaking down the user story into specific tasks and sub-tasks in action. Within daily scrum, two points that need to be discussed would be what have team members contributed to the sprint goal, and specific roadblocks that may impede the teams work. In sprint review, two points that would need to be discussed would be reviewing the timeline, budget, and capabilities of the next releases of the product, and collaborating/planning on next steps. 
An 'Epic' is used to name and provide the definition of the topic of the task at hand. 'Stories' are used to plan bigger projects within the task. 'Tasks' are single, more narrower to-dos within the project, and finally, 'Bugs' are issues that are causing problems in the product, or slowing down progress within the task.
